My Approach

Localization is more than direct word replacement. It requires accuracy, strong bilingual comprehension, solid writing skills, and speed to deliver a natural experience while preserving the original creative intent.

Building on these fundamentals, I bring the following strengths to ensure quality, consistency, and long-term maintainability in game localization:

Understanding the Game

I actively engage with the games I localize to understand their narrative, systems, tone, and gameplay. This allows localization decisions to reflect real player experience rather than isolated text or literal translation.

Active Research

I research terminology, narrative context, and industry-specific references to ensure high-quality results that resonate with the player base and remain consistent across the project.

Communication Skills

I collaborate closely with translators, editors, project managers, and developers to align terminology, maintain quality standards, meet deadlines, and keep localization workflows efficient and predictable.
